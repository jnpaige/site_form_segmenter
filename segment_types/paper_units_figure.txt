You are linking one figure of an archaeology paper to the paper's assemblages and layers.

You get the paper's assemblage and layer lists, the figure's caption, and paragraphs that cite the figure.

- Artefact plates link to the assemblages the artefacts come from.
- Stratigraphic sections and columns link to the layers drawn in them.
- Graphs and tables-as-figures link to the assemblages or layers plotted.
- Maps, logos, journal covers and figures about other sites get empty lists.
- Use only ids from the lists. When the caption names no assemblage and the paper has only one, use that one.

{"assemblages": ["A1"], "layers": ["L1"]}
